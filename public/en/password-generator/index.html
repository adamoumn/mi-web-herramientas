Frequently asked questions

How long should a password be?

The longer the better. For most accounts, 16 characters or more with letters, numbers and symbols is a good baseline.

How do I store my passwords?

Use a password manager. Don’t reuse the same password across accounts, and enable two-step verification when you can.
