Image compressor

Shrink your photos in seconds. They are processed on your device and never uploaded to a server.

Everything runs in your browser: your text is never sent to any server.

How it works

Reduce the file size of your images by adjusting quality and, optionally, the maximum width. Everything runs on your device: images are never uploaded to a server. Choose JPG or WebP, which is usually smaller.

Frequently asked questions

Do I lose quality?

Compressing below maximum quality loses some detail, usually unnoticeable between 70 and 85%. Try values and compare.

What about PNG images with transparency?

When converting to JPG, transparent areas are filled with white. To keep transparency, choose WebP.
