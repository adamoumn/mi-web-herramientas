It is an estimate, not proof. No detector is 100% reliable: it can flag human-written text as AI and miss AI-made text. Do not use it as the only reason to accuse anyone.

Do not paste personal data or confidential information. Always review the result before using it.

How it works

We analyze each sentence looking for typical signs of AI-generated text: generic tone, very symmetrical structures, repeated transitions and a lack of concrete details. The overall percentage is the average of the sentences, weighted by length.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my own text show as AI?

Very formal, neutral or tidily written texts can look like AI even when a person wrote them. That is why the result is only indicative.

What do the colors mean?

Underlined text marks the sentences most likely to be AI. Yellow marks sentences with a medium probability.
