Text cleaner

Remove extra spaces and line breaks, delete duplicate lines and sort your lists in one click.

Everything runs in your browser: your text is never sent to any server.

How it works

Clean text pasted from PDFs, emails or web pages: remove double spaces, empty lines and unnecessary line breaks, delete duplicate lines or sort them alphabetically. Useful for lists, copying from PDFs or preparing data.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text saved?

No. Everything is processed in your browser.

What is “join into one paragraph” for?

When you copy text from a PDF, each line is often cut short. This option joins it into one continuous paragraph.
