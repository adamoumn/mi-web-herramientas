How it works

This tool counts the words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences and paragraphs of your text in real time, and estimates the reading time at 200 words per minute. It is handy for word-limited essays, social media posts, descriptions and SEO copy.

Frequently asked questions

Is my text saved?

No. Everything is counted in your browser and your text is never sent to any server.

How are words counted?

A word is any group of characters separated by spaces. Other tools may count hyphens or symbols slightly differently.
