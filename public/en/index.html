Frequently asked questions

Are they really free?

Yes. You can use them without paying or creating an account. If demand is high, we may set daily usage limits.

Do you store my text?

We do not store the content of your texts. They are sent to a third-party AI service only to generate the result. Even so, do not paste personal data or confidential information.

Is the AI detector 100% accurate?

No. No detector is. It gives a rough estimate and can be wrong, both flagging human text as AI and the other way around.

Can I use the generated text?

Yes, but always review it. AI can make mistakes or sound generic, and you are responsible for how you use the text.
