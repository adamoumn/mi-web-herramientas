How to humanize ChatGPT text so it doesn’t sound like a robot

AI text is usually correct, but you can tell. Here are the signals and how to fix them.

Why AI text sounds like AI

It is not that it has mistakes. It is that almost everything sounds the same: balanced sentences of similar length, repeated connectors (“moreover”, “in conclusion”, “it is important to note”), generic vocabulary and paragraphs that always follow the same structure. What is usually missing is the most human part: a concrete example, an opinion or a detail only someone who lived it would know.

Six steps to humanize it

  1. Read it aloud. If a sentence sounds odd or too solemn, a person would not say it.
  2. Cut the filler. Delete connectors that add nothing and openers like “in today’s world”.
  3. Vary the rhythm. Mix long sentences with very short ones. Human writing does not move at a steady beat.
  4. Add something of your own. An example, an anecdote, a figure you know. It is what most separates personal writing from generic text.
  5. Break the symmetry. Not every section needs three points or the same shape.
  6. Check the facts. AI sometimes invents numbers, names or sources. Verify them before publishing.
