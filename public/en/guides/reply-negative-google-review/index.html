How to reply to a negative Google review (with an example)

A good reply does not only convince the angry customer: everyone who checks your business afterwards reads it too.

Why it matters so much

People looking for your restaurant, clinic or shop usually read the reviews, and also how you answer the bad ones. A calm, professional reply can build more trust than having no complaints at all.

Structure of a good reply

  1. Thank them and personalize. Use their name if it appears and thank them for sharing their experience.
  2. Apologize without excuses. A sincere apology works better than justifying yourself.
  3. Mention the specific problem. It shows you read the review and it is not a template.
  4. Offer a solution and move it to private. Invite them to contact you directly to sort it out.
  5. Close kindly. End by thanking them for the chance to improve.

An example to adapt

“Hi, thank you for sharing your experience. We are very sorry the wait was so long on Saturday; that is not the service we want to give. We are reviewing how we organize weekend shifts. We would like to talk to you to make it right: write to us at [your email] and we will sort it out. Thank you for giving us the chance to improve.”

Mistakes to avoid

  • Arguing with or doubting the customer in public.
  • Sharing the customer’s personal data or case details.
  • Promising compensation you cannot deliver.
  • Waiting weeks: ideally reply within a day or two.
  • Pasting the same reply on every review.

If the review is fake or breaks Google’s rules, you can report it from your business profile.
