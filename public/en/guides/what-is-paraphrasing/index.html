What is paraphrasing and how to do it right without plagiarizing

Rewording an idea is not enough if you do not understand it or cite where it comes from.

What paraphrasing is

It means expressing someone else’s idea in your own words and your own structure while keeping the meaning. It is not swapping a few synonyms and leaving the rest the same: that stays too close to the original and can count as plagiarism.

How to do it well

  1. Understand the text. If you cannot explain it aloud, you cannot paraphrase it yet.
  2. Close the original. Write the idea from memory, as you would tell a friend.
  3. Change the structure, not just the words: the order of ideas, the type of sentence, the focus.
  4. Compare. Check you have not changed the meaning or left sentences too similar.
  5. Cite the source. Paraphrasing does not exempt you from saying where the idea comes from.

When a tool helps

A paraphraser is useful to improve your own writing, avoid repetition or adapt the tone (more formal, simpler, shorter). In ours you can choose the style and see new words in green. It is a starting point: always review the result and, if you use other people’s ideas, cite them.
