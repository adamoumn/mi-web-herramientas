Case converter

Convert your text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case or Sentence case in one click.

Everything runs in your browser: your text is never sent to any server.

How it works

Convert your text to uppercase, lowercase, title case or sentence case with one click. Ideal for fixing text typed with caps lock on, headlines, or lists pasted from other places.

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle accents and special letters?

Yes. The conversion follows the language rules, so accented and special letters are kept.

What does “Title Case” do?

It capitalizes the first letter of every word. For regular sentences, “Sentence case” is usually what you want.
