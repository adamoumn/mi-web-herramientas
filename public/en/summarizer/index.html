How it works

The tool reads your text, identifies the main ideas and condenses them without adding information that is not in the original. Choose between a paragraph, a list of key points or a very short summary.

Frequently asked questions

How long can the text be?

From 40 to 3,000 words. For a longer text, split it into parts and summarize each one.

Can the summary be wrong?

It can miss nuances or make mistakes, like any AI. If the summary matters, check it against the original.
